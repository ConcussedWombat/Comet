## Benchmark / constraints
Default-off independent switches; benchmark owner A/B baseline with same mip skip, raw mirror, recheck and scene. Compare each then combined, keep first frame and 120/300 captures, 5-minute waiting (total/due/partial/away), recovered interior count and bytes, sparse scheduled partial count, remap overflows/rescan size, new/retry serviced, texture pass phase ms, local VRAM/budget, CPU/GPU time/fps and black albedo. Capture shader/slot/address for visible City textures if recovered. No new admission past room(), no forced mip promotion, no destructive residency changes. These recover work wrongly skipped/starved or invalidations lost; they do not solve physical VRAM capacity alone. Actual texture data can only be uploaded when retained mip span contains usable bytes; a positive guest-span observation does not guarantee retained mip residency. No build/game run by N5; stage source diff only. Test sparse VirtualAlloc interior/tail/empty and guard-safe behavior, producer overlap/overflow, queue first-look fairness and write-generation tests. Expected logs, not measured: N5 DexNom sparse residency recovered, N5 DexNom sparse partial upload scheduled, N5 DexNom remap overflow rescan, N5 DexNom fair texture queue.
