## 465b94f — scattered indirect gather (B4)

README lines 48–104: Windows 11, i7-14700KF, RTX 4070 12 GB, first rooms ~3,800 draws/frame at 2560x1440. With previous changes and CARD_REHASH_RUNS=1, 104 → 97 ms/frame (9.6 → 10.3 fps); ~600 of ~1,600 draws missing windows gathered. 7.9 million gathered records compared against fresh GPU clamps, zero differences reported. Upstream argument buffer ~100 MB; offsets between draws 16–33,056 bytes forward or backward. Gather addresses scattered records, not just contiguous windows. At 97 ms/frame card busy ~87 ms; layer's own lists ~19 ms; clamps 10–20 ms; title's ~30 compute passes and geometry ~50 ms. Categories as described upstream; do not add overlapping counters as independent savings. The 7 ms gain is conditional on the other switches, not the 0.8-to-10.3 fps gap.
